Gujarati New Year

નૂતન વર્ષાભિનંદન

In Gujarat, we celebrate the New Year as Bestu Varas.

It marks a fresh beginning, a new year filled with hopes, blessings and happiness.

Families visit relatives, seek blessings from elders, wear new clothes and exchange good wishes.

A new beginning. A new chapter. A new reason to smile. ❤️

Our Little Traditions 🪔

🌸 Rangoli & Diyas

We decorate our homes with colourful rangoli, flowers and diyas. They represent positivity, light and welcoming good things.

🙏 Blessings from Elders

We visit family members and elders, take their blessings and wish everyone happiness and prosperity.

📖 Chopda Pujan

Many Gujarati business families worship their account books and begin a fresh financial year with prayers for success.

🍚 Annakut

A beautiful offering of different food items to God, expressing gratitude and devotion.
