Gujarati New Year ❤️

નૂતન વર્ષાભિનંદન

In Gujarat, we celebrate Gujarati New Year as Bestu Varas.

It marks a fresh beginning, a new year filled with hopes, blessings and happiness.

Families visit relatives, seek blessings from elders, wear new clothes and exchange good wishes.

A new beginning. A new chapter. A new reason to smile. ❤️

Let me show you something 🥰

🪔

1. Rangoli & Diyas

We decorate our homes with colourful rangoli, flowers and beautiful diyas. They represent positivity, light and welcoming good things into our lives.

🙏

2. Blessings from Elders

We visit our family members and elders, take their blessings and wish everyone happiness and prosperity.

📖

3. Chopda Pujan

Many Gujarati business families worship their account books and begin a fresh financial year with prayers for success and prosperity.

🍚

4. Annakut

A beautiful offering of different food items to God, expressing gratitude and devotion.

One more cute Gujarati thing 😋

🍬

Sweet Beginnings

Festivals are incomplete without sweets and delicious food!

We share sweets with family and friends and celebrate together.

🧂

Sabras

In some Gujarati families, salt is bought on New Year as a symbol of welcoming all the flavours of life.

Because life should have happiness, sweetness, challenges and beautiful memories.

And honestly, Cherry... every celebration is better when shared with someone special. ❤️
